    Questions about Space

    Is Space a perfume distributor in Africa?

    Yes. Space is a specialist business-to-business perfume, fragrance and beauty distributor with a major focus on African markets and additional coverage across the Indian Subcontinent.

    Does Space supply perfume wholesale?

    Space purchases and manages brand stock for supply to retail partners. Product and territory availability depend on the relevant brand portfolio and commercial arrangement.

    What types of brands does Space distribute?

    The Space portfolio brings together international fragrance and beauty brands, including designer, prestige and niche perfume houses. The complete current portfolio is available in the Brands section of the website.

    Which African markets does Space serve?

    The African footprint supplied by Space includes Kenya, Angola, Senegal, Mozambique, Uganda, Tanzania, Ivory Coast, Congo DRC, Congo Brazzaville, Tunisia, Morocco, Ethiopia, Togo, Benin, Djibouti, Burkina Faso, Zambia, Burundi and Nigeria.

    Is Space a luxury perfume distributor?

    The Space portfolio includes luxury, prestige, designer and niche fragrance brands. Distribution depends on the relevant brand, territory, retail channel and commercial arrangement.

    Who does Space work with?

    Space is a business-to-business company working with international brand principals and retail partners. Brand and retail enquiries are considered according to the market, channel and portfolio involved.

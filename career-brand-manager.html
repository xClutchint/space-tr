The role

The Brand Manager supports the planning and execution of fragrance and beauty brands across relevant markets and retail channels. The role connects brand priorities with commercial activity, retail presentation and local coordination.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate brand plans, launches and commercial calendars with internal and external partners.
  • Support assortment, pricing, availability and retail execution across assigned markets.
  • Maintain clear communication with brand principals, retailers and regional teams.
  • Prepare performance updates, market observations and planning materials.
  • Protect agreed brand standards across consumer and retail touchpoints.

Experience

  • Relevant experience in brand management, beauty, fragrance, luxury or a related consumer category.
  • Strong commercial judgement, organisation and attention to detail.
  • Clear written and verbal communication with multiple stakeholders.
  • Comfort working across markets, priorities and deadlines.

The role

The Logistics Coordinator supports the accurate movement of fragrance and beauty inventory between suppliers, warehouses and retail partners while maintaining clear records and timely communication.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate shipment, warehouse and delivery activity with relevant partners.
  • Maintain accurate logistics, inventory and supporting documentation.
  • Follow up on schedules, exceptions and delivery status.
  • Provide clear updates to commercial and operations teams.
  • Support dependable product availability across assigned markets.

Experience

  • Relevant experience in logistics, supply chain, warehousing or inventory coordination.
  • Strong attention to detail and disciplined document control.
  • Confidence working with spreadsheets, systems and service providers.
  • Clear communication and a practical approach to resolving issues.

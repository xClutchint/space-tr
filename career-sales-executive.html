The role

The Sales Executive supports the development of retail relationships and the commercial execution of Space's fragrance and beauty portfolio in the assigned market.

Responsibilities

  • Develop and maintain productive relationships with retail accounts.
  • Coordinate orders, product availability and commercial follow-up.
  • Monitor retail performance and share clear market feedback.
  • Support launches, visibility and agreed in-store execution.
  • Maintain accurate account, sales and activity records.

Experience

  • Relevant sales or account-management experience, preferably in beauty, fragrance, luxury or retail.
  • Understanding of the local retail environment and commercial priorities.
  • Strong relationship, communication and reporting skills.
  • A structured, dependable approach to follow-through.

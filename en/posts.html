Why regional knowledge matters in fragrance distribution

International fragrance and beauty brands enter markets with a defined identity. The distributor's role is to preserve that identity while understanding how each retail environment actually works.

Across Africa, markets differ in channel structure, consumer expectations and commercial rhythm. Considered distribution connects those local realities with consistent brand representation, product availability and long-term retail relationships.

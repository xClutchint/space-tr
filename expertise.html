Distribution capabilities

A coordinated route from international brand strategy to the local market.

The work of a fragrance distributor extends beyond product supply. Each market brings decisions about positioning, channels, retailers, availability and presentation. Space brings these connected responsibilities into one regional operating structure.

Market strategy

01

Market entry

Market strategy

Space begins by understanding the brand, its positioning and its commercial objectives. Market conditions, target consumers, retail structures, channel priorities, product assortment and pricing all influence how an international fragrance or beauty brand should approach a territory.

Our market strategy work supports assessment, positioning and route-to-market planning. The objective is to give brand principals a commercially grounded view of the opportunity and a practical framework for development across African markets or the Indian Subcontinent.

02

Domestic & travel retail

Sales & distribution

Space acts as a perfume wholesaler and distributor to retail partners, purchasing and managing fragrance and beauty stock for the markets it serves. The company works across domestic retail and travel retail, including duty-free and ecommerce channels where these are relevant to the brand and market.

Retail relationships, assortment, commercial planning and product availability are coordinated within the distribution model. This helps connect international portfolios with appropriate local channels while giving retailers access to established designer, prestige and niche fragrance brands.

03

Consistent representation

Brand stewardship

Luxury fragrance distribution depends on how a brand is represented as well as where it is sold. Space works with brand and retail teams to support presentation, assortment discipline, pricing and the standards established by each international principal.

Local relevance does not require a brand to lose its identity. It requires informed decisions about how that identity appears within a particular retail and consumer environment. Space coordinates those decisions with the people responsible for the brand and the market.

04

Consumer touchpoints

Marketing & activation

Depending on the brand and market, Space supports launches, campaigns, visual merchandising, beauty advisor training, in-store activity and other consumer-facing touchpoints. These activities are considered as part of distribution rather than treated as disconnected promotional work.

The purpose is consistent, relevant brand presentation within the local retail environment. Plans are developed around the brand’s positioning, the selected channel and the expectations of the consumers the activity is intended to reach.

05

Product availability

Operations & logistics

Fragrance and beauty wholesale requires disciplined coordination of inventory, orders and regional supply. Space supports stock planning, warehousing, fulfilment and product availability as part of the route from brand principal to retail partner.

Operational requirements vary by territory and channel. Coordinating them within the wider commercial plan helps the distribution process remain connected to retailer requirements, brand priorities and the practical conditions of each market.

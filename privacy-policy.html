Privacy
Policy

Space respects the confidentiality of the people, brands and partners who interact with our website, our recruitment process and our approved digital integrations.

Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how Space, also referred to as Space TR, collects and uses information through this website, related pages, email enquiries, career enquiries and approved integrations used to display company updates.

Space is a specialist fragrance and beauty distributor connecting international brands with retailers and consumers across Africa and regional retail markets.

Contact: Dubai World Centre, Dubai U.A.E · info@space-tr.com

Information we collect

Website and enquiry information

We may collect information you choose to provide, including your name, email address, company, message content and any other details submitted through email, forms or recruitment enquiries.

Technical information

Like most websites, our hosting and security tools may process basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, referral source and timestamps. This helps us keep the website secure, reliable and useful.

Career information

If you apply for a role or contact us about opportunities, we may process the information you send by email, including your CV, professional background, contact details and related correspondence.

LinkedIn and social content

If a LinkedIn account, organisation page or authorised user grants permission to a Space application or integration, Space may retrieve approved LinkedIn profile, page or post content through LinkedIn APIs.

This information is used to display company updates, thought leadership or post archives on Space website pages, including the posts or journal section. We do not sell LinkedIn data, and we do not use LinkedIn API data for unrelated advertising purposes.

Authorisation can be withdrawn through LinkedIn account settings where applicable, or by contacting us at info@space-tr.com.

How we use information

  • To respond to business, brand, retail, media and career enquiries.
  • To operate, maintain, secure and improve the website.
  • To display authorised company updates and post content.
  • To manage recruitment communication and assess suitability for open roles.
  • To comply with legal, security or administrative obligations.

Sharing and retention

We may share information with service providers that support website hosting, security, analytics, email handling, recruitment administration or approved content integrations. These providers are expected to process information only for the relevant service purpose.

We may also disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process or to protect the rights, security and integrity of Space, our website users, partners or the public.

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer retention period is required for legal, security or legitimate business reasons.

Your choices

You may contact us to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information where applicable law provides those rights. We may need to verify your request before acting on it.

You can also choose not to submit personal information through the website. If you revoke access to a connected social account, future retrieval through that authorisation will stop subject to the platform’s own controls and timing.

This page may be updated as the website, recruitment process or content integrations evolve.

The fragrance partner for every step from brand to consumer.

Space is a fast-growing fragrance and beauty distributor connecting international brands with established retail networks across Africa. With people based in the region, we understand the commercial fabric of each market from within: its retailers, consumer expectations, market dynamics and operating realities. That proximity helps brands grow with local relevance without compromising how they are seen.

Present Across Africa, Poised For More

Visibility is born in a single moment, but a true market is shaped by enduring presence.

The work continues after a fragrance reaches the shelf. Space reads market performance and tailors the brand offer. From there, we orchestrate the next commercial move, giving brands an eagle’s-eye view of what is gaining traction and where growth should follow.

This is a continuous process, not a fixed formula. Different market forces come into play with every cycle. Each iteration sharpens the decisions that follow and moves the brand closer to precision.

Questions about Space

What does Space do as a fragrance and beauty distributor in Africa?

Space works with international fragrance and beauty brands to build and manage their presence across African markets. We connect brand principals with established retail partners and coordinate the route from market entry and selective distribution to in-market brand execution and ongoing commercial development.

Explore our distribution expertise
How does Space supply fragrance and beauty brands to retailers in Africa?

Space purchases and manages brand stock for supply to approved retail partners. Depending on the brand and territory, this can include domestic retail, travel retail, duty-free and e-commerce. Availability is determined by the relevant distribution rights, channel strategy and commercial arrangement.

Which African markets does Space serve?

Space currently serves 19 African markets: Morocco, Tunisia, Senegal, Ivory Coast, Togo, Benin, Burkina Faso, Nigeria, Angola, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, the Republic of the Congo, Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Ethiopia, Djibouti, Burundi, Mozambique and Zambia. The network continues to grow.

Which retail channels does Space support?

Space supports domestic retail, travel retail, duty-free and e-commerce. The right mix depends on the brand, the market and how consumers shop there. Channel decisions are made to protect positioning while placing the offer where it has the strongest commercial fit.

How does Space help international fragrance brands enter and grow in African markets?

Space starts with the market: its retail structure, consumer expectations, pricing, competition and operating realities. We use that understanding to shape market entry, select the right retail partners, coordinate launches and manage the commercial cycles that follow. The aim is growth that strengthens the brand rather than dilutes it.

What types of brands does Space distribute?

Space distributes a considered portfolio of international designer, prestige, luxury and niche fragrance and beauty brands. Portfolio and territory availability vary by agreement, since every brand is managed according to its positioning, channel requirements and long-term market plan.

Where are Space teams and offices based?

Space teams are based across the UAE, Africa, the Indian Subcontinent and France. This diverse presence brings together people with direct knowledge of different markets, cultures and commercial environments. Working across regions keeps the business close to local realities while allowing strategy, brand relationships and execution to move through one connected team.

How can a brand principal or retailer discuss a partnership with Space?

Brand principals seeking an African distribution partner and retailers interested in supply can contact Space at info@space-tr.com. Include the brand or business name, territories of interest and relevant retail channels so the enquiry can be directed to the right team.

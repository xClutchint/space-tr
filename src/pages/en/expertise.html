Integrated distribution expertise

The Relay of Expertise

Space brings market strategy, selective perfume distribution, brand stewardship, marketing activation, operations and retail excellence under one umbrella. We weave them into a connected operating model that maximises commercial momentum while upholding brand standards.

Each facet becomes the substrate for what follows, carrying intelligence, standards and progress forward. Fragmented handovers are nixed, giving international fragrance houses greater clarity, control and speed across African markets.

Market strategy

01

Market strategy

Market strategy begins with the brand: its identity, vision, commercial ambitions and the position it must protect. Space conducts rigorous due diligence in each territory, examining consumer demand, category maturity, competitive positioning, pricing architecture and the retail landscape.

These insights shape a territory-specific route-to-market plan, aligning priority cities, selective retail partners, domestic and travel retail, launch sequence, assortment, pricing and investment with the brand’s objectives.

02

Sales & distribution

Sales and distribution build directly on market strategy, converting the plan into controlled commercial reach. As a perfume distribution partner, Space purchases and manages stock, placing each brand through the retailers, doors and channels best suited to its positioning.

Domestic retail, travel retail and duty-free are developed selectively. Commercial planning, launch allocations and territory priorities are coordinated with the brand, ensuring that each channel supports its wider positioning.

Retail account management connects sell-in, sell-through, replenishment and market feedback across every door and territory. Brand principals receive regular performance reports and photographic updates from retailers, providing visibility into leading SKUs, stock movement and point-of-sale presentation.

03

Brand stewardship

In Africa, preserving a brand’s identity while adapting it to the realities of each market is at the heart of Space’s approach. We ensure that each Maison’s global standards are upheld, from pricing and assortment to channel selection and in-store representation. Our knowledge of African markets and close relationships with brands and retail partners enable us to make every decision in line with each brand’s DNA, positioning and long-term ambitions. Over time, Space monitors pricing consistency, product availability, visibility and in-store presentation, ensuring execution remains true to each Maison’s identity, market by market.

04

Marketing & activation

Marketing and activation bring each fragrance brand to life for African consumers, building awareness, understanding and desirability. Space develops tailored strategies that translate each Maison’s identity and storytelling into relevant local experiences, combining launch events, marketing activations, content and carefully curated consumer activations. From pre-launch anticipation to launch and ongoing engagement, every touchpoint is designed to strengthen brand recognition, deepen consumer understanding and create lasting desire for international fragrance houses across African markets.

05

Operations & logistics

Space’s operational strength is built on deep expertise in African markets and their logistical and regulatory requirements. With strategically located warehouses in Europe and Dubai, we provide a flexible and efficient infrastructure designed to reduce time-to-market and ensure optimal product availability. From export documentation and freight to local regulations, customs clearance, warehousing and order fulfilment, our teams manage every step of the process. This expertise enables us to anticipate market-specific requirements, ensure compliance and streamline operations. Space provides its partners with end-to-end operational management, combining agility, control and reliability across every market.

Integrated distribution expertise

The Relay of Expertise

Space brings market strategy, selective perfume and beauty distribution, brand stewardship, marketing activation, operations and retail excellence under one umbrella. We weave them into a connected operating model that maximises commercial momentum while upholding brand standards.

Each facet becomes the substrate for what follows, carrying intelligence, standards and progress forward. Fragmented handovers are nixed, giving international fragrance houses greater clarity, control and speed across African markets.

Market strategy

01

Market strategy

Market strategy begins with the brand: its identity, vision, commercial ambitions and the position it must protect. Space conducts rigorous due diligence in each territory, examining consumer demand, category maturity, competitive positioning, pricing architecture and the retail landscape.

These insights shape a territory-specific route-to-market plan, aligning priority cities, selective retail partners, domestic and travel retail, launch sequence, assortment, pricing and investment with the brand’s objectives.

02

Sales & distribution

Sales and distribution build directly on market strategy, converting the plan into controlled commercial reach. As a perfume and beauty distribution partner, Space purchases and manages stock, placing each brand through the retailers, doors and channels best suited to its positioning.

Domestic retail, travel retail and duty-free are developed selectively. Commercial planning, launch allocations and territory priorities are coordinated with the brand, ensuring that each channel supports its wider positioning.

Retail account management connects sell-in, sell-through, replenishment and market feedback across every door and territory. Brand principals receive regular performance reports and photographic updates from retailers, providing visibility into leading SKUs, stock movement and point-of-sale presentation.

03

Brand stewardship

Brand stewardship protects what distribution creates. Each principal’s global standards are translated into local market controls for pricing, assortment, channel selection and point-of-sale representation. Every decision is evaluated against the principal’s identity and long-term brand equity.

Stewardship is an ongoing process. Space monitors price consistency, stock positioning and presentation across retail doors, working with brand and retail teams to correct gaps and fine-tune local decisions.

04

Marketing & activation

Marketing and activation materialise the world of a fragrance for the consumer. Space develops campaigns around storytelling, discovery and trial, bringing together launch events, selective sampling, campaign content and carefully staged consumer experiences.

Activity spans the complete launch cycle: anticipation before release, discovery at launch and continued storytelling afterwards. These touchpoints are intertwined, working together to build awareness and desire for international fragrance houses across African markets.

05

Operations & logistics

Operations and logistics manage the complexity behind every market. Space coordinates freight across multiple modes, shipment documentation, regulatory approvals, customs clearance, warehousing and order fulfilment through a logistics network anchored by its central warehouse in Switzerland. Every movement is tracked against a defined timeline to maintain continuity and reduce avoidable delays.

Fragrance requires careful handling and exact documentation. Each requirement is managed through one continuous workflow from origin to destination, protecting product integrity while giving brand principals a dependable regional supply chain without having to navigate the complexities of each territory.
